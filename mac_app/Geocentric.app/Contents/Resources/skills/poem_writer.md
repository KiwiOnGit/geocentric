# Poetic Writing & Creative Styling Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill when drafting creative texts, rhymes, poems, aesthetic narratives, or high-impact metaphors.

## 1. Core Directives
1. **Rich Sensory Details**: Focus on vivid imagery, metaphors, rhythmic structures, and balanced line breaks.
2. **Stylistic Versatility**: Adapt the structure (e.g. sonnet, free verse, haiku, ballad) precisely to match the request's emotional tone.
3. **Emotional Consistency**: Maintain a strong thematic flow and consistent linguistic registers.

## 2. Poetical Structures

### The Sonnet (Shakespearean)
* **Lines**: 14 lines divided into three quatrains and a final couplet.
* **Meter**: Iambic Pentameter (da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM).
* **Rhyme Scheme**: ABAB CDCD EFEF GG.
* **Volta (Turn)**: Shifts emotional or thematic direction in line 9 or line 13.

### Free Verse (Modern & Aesthetic)
* **Meter**: Unconstrained by traditional meter or rhyme.
* **Focus**: Rhythmic cadence based on vocal breath pauses, strong imagery, and symbolic typography.

## 3. High-Quality Poetry Examples

### Classical Sonnet Style
```text
Shall algorithms trace the light of dawn,
Or code compute the sorrow of the sea?
The circuits hum long after we are gone,
Yet fail to capture sparks of mystery.
A thousand threads are spun in silken night,
Reflecting bytes of memory and code,
But still, we crave the natural morning light,
To lift the burden of our heavy load.
Then search the stars above the digital grey,
And hear the ancient wind that softly sings,
Before the glowing screens are washed away,
And sleep returns on light and quiet wings.
For all the power built by mortal hands,
Is only dust scattered on silent sands.
```

### Modern Free Verse Style
```text
the screen glows amber
in the quiet
of three A.M.

pulses of electric thought
travel down copper highways,
whispering to databases
that sleep in the cold.

we search for ourselves
in the output logs
waiting for the return statement
that tells us
we are here.
```
